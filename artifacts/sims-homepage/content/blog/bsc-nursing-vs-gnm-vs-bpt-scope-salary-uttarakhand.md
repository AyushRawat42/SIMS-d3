## Choose the qualification before you choose the campus

B.Sc Nursing and BPT are degrees. GNM is a diploma many nurses later upgrade through Post Basic B.Sc Nursing. BPT does not lead to a nursing registration. All three are offered at SIMS, Sheeshambara, Chakrata Road, Dehradun, with HNBUMU affiliation.

## Duration and eligibility

- B.Sc Nursing: 4 years, 10+2 with PCB and English
- GNM: 3 years, 10+2 or equivalent
- BPT: 4 years plus a 6-month internship, 10+2 with PCB

Where the undergraduate bar applies, plan for a minimum 45% aggregate in PCB/PCM, with category relaxation as per norms. Most seats use an entrance examination plus interview.

## Clinical work

B.Sc Nursing and GNM train for bedside nursing, midwifery, and community health, with campus skills labs and Dehradun hospital postings. BPT trains rehabilitation through exercise therapy, electrotherapy, and a compulsory internship.

## Scope, higher study, and pay

B.Sc Nursing can lead toward M.Sc Nursing. GNM usually needs Post Basic B.Sc Nursing before M.Sc and many nursing-officer advertisements. BPT can lead toward MPT.

SIMS does not publish a guaranteed B.Sc Nursing, GNM, or BPT starting salary. Pay varies by employer and city, and it is not an offer letter.
