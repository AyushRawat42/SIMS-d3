## What HNBUMU counseling means for SIMS applicants

Hemwati Nandan Bahuguna Uttarakhand Medical Education University (HNBUMU) is the affiliating university for SIMS programmes in Dehradun. Affiliation governs the curriculum, the examinations, and the degree or diploma printed on the certificate. Counseling is the later step where a rank, an entrance result, or qualifying marks are matched to a course and a campus. It does not replace Indian Nursing Council recognition or state nursing-council registration after a nursing course, and it does not replace the paramedical council pathway for laboratory or imaging programmes.

Families in Dehradun, the rest of Uttarakhand, and neighbouring states often meet the university name for the first time on an admit card or a counseling SMS. Treat that message as a deadline, not as a college offer. Read the seat matrix, the fee to be paid at reporting, and the institute name before anyone travels. Sushila Institute of Medical Sciences is at Sheeshambara, Sighniwala, Central Hope Town, Chakrata Road, Dehradun – 248197. When a list names the institute, match it to the course you actually want: B.Sc Nursing, GNM, Post Basic B.Sc Nursing, BPT, BMLT, or BMRIT.

This guide walks through registration, eligibility, the document file, choice filling, allotment rounds, and reporting. Button labels on the HNBUMU portal change from session to session. Use the steps below as a checklist of decisions, then follow the click-path printed on the notice for your year.

## Step 1 — Registration on the counseling notice

Registration is the first paid or login step on the counseling notice, and it is separate from the SIMS application conversation you may already have had. Open only the link printed on the HNBUMU or institute notice for that intake. Create the account with the same name, date of birth, and mobile number that appear on the 10th certificate and the entrance application. A mismatch here is what delays document verification later, even when the marks are fine.

Keep the registration number, the password, and the payment receipt in one folder with the admit card. If the notice asks for a counseling fee, pay it inside the window and download the receipt before the portal session ends. Screenshot a confirmation page as a backup, then save the PDF. Parents who share one phone among siblings should use the candidate’s own number when the notice says the OTP will be used again at choice locking.

About 75% of SIMS seats are filled through an All India entrance examination plus an institute interview. A notified share of NRI or sponsored seats may use qualifying-examination marks plus an interview. Register in the pool you are actually eligible for. Do not assume a private-college seat skips the university schedule. If the notice and the SIMS admissions page disagree on a date, ask admissions in writing before you lock a choice. The published admissions phone is +91 9759761244.

## Step 2 — Confirm eligibility before you pay a seat fee

Undergraduate pathways at SIMS generally expect a recognised 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology or Mathematics, and a minimum 45% aggregate in PCB/PCM, with category relaxation as per norms. B.Sc Nursing also expects English as published on the programme page. GNM follows 10+2 or equivalent as per the admission guidelines. Post Basic B.Sc Nursing is for GNM holders who already have nursing registration. BPT expects 12th PCB. BMLT and BMRIT follow the science-stream rules on their programme pages.

Age limits are not published as one campus-wide number. Use the age rule on the current HNBUMU and institute notice for your category, and calculate age from the date of birth on the 10th certificate. Reserved categories, including economically weaker sections within the entrance pool and bona fide Uttarakhand candidates, follow the seat matrix in that year’s notice. A category certificate that is valid for a school scholarship is not automatically valid for a medical-education seat. Check the issuing authority named in the notice.

If your 12th board is still declaring results when registration opens, read whether the notice allows an undertaking or demands a mark sheet on the day you register. Carry the internet mark sheet only if the notice says it is accepted at verification. Originals are still required at reporting.

## Step 3 — Document checklist for verification

Counseling desks reject incomplete files more often than they reject a slightly lower percentage. Build one original set and one photocopy set, in the order the notice lists, before choice filling opens. The exact list is the one on the active notice. The items below are the set SIMS admissions already asks families to prepare for nursing and paramedical verification in Dehradun.

- 10th mark sheet and certificate, used for date of birth and name
- 12th (10+2) mark sheet and passing certificate, with PCB or PCM subjects visible
- HNBUMU or entrance admit card, plus the rank card or result PDF if the notice asks for it
- Uttarakhand domicile certificate when you are claiming a state or bona fide seat
- Category certificate (SC, ST, OBC, or EWS) in the format and from the authority named in the notice, only if you claim that seat
- Photo identity proof that matches the application
- Transfer, migration, or school-leaving certificate if the notice asks for it
- Passport-size photographs matching the application
- For Post Basic B.Sc Nursing: GNM diploma, mark sheets, and State Nursing Council RN/RM registration

## Step 4 — Choice filling and how to lock SIMS Dehradun

Choice filling is the list of institute-plus-course pairs you are willing to join, usually in the order you prefer. Follow the steps printed on that year’s HNBUMU counseling notice. This page does not invent a button name. Before you lock, confirm three things: the institute name is Sushila Institute of Medical Sciences, Dehradun; the course name matches the programme you visited; and the seat category (entrance, interview, or NRI/sponsored) is the one you are eligible for. A similar college name from another campus is a different choice.

Put the course decision ahead of the campus decision. If you want a nursing degree after 12th PCB, lock B.Sc Nursing at SIMS rather than GNM. If you want the shorter diploma and are willing to add Post Basic B.Sc Nursing later, lock GNM. If you already hold GNM and registration, look at Post Basic B.Sc Nursing. If you want physiotherapy, choose BPT, which includes a compulsory internship. BMLT and BMRIT are the laboratory and imaging routes. Filling every course “just in case” can allot a programme you did not budget for, because a higher choice that is offered is the one you are expected to take.

Lock the list only after a second person has read the institute spelling and the course name aloud. Save or print the locked-choice receipt. Visit or ask for a lab walk-through before you treat the lock as final. SIMS describes clinical learning through campus skills labs and hospital postings in its Dehradun tie-up network. Ask for the current recognition or affiliation letter for your intake year rather than relying on a brochure line.

## Step 5 — Seat allotment rounds

Allotment is the university’s answer to your locked list. A round offers one seat, or none, according to rank, category, and the choices still available. The notice will say how many rounds are planned and whether a later round is a mop-up or a stray-vacancy round. Dates move. What stays useful is the decision you must make after each result: accept and report, or follow the upgrade rule if the notice allows you to wait for a higher choice.

Read the allotment letter for the institute, the course, the category of the seat, the reporting deadline, and the fee to be paid that day. An offer for SIMS Dehradun is specific to Sheeshambara. If the letter names a different institute, do not travel to Chakrata Road expecting that seat to transfer. If you are allotted a course you no longer want, use the withdrawal or free-exit rule in the notice if one exists. Skipping reporting without reading that rule can forfeit a fee or block a later round.

Keep the admit card and the registration receipt with the allotment PDF. Verification desks ask for the same identity that sat the entrance exam. Reach Dehradun with time to spare on reporting day. Chakrata Road traffic and document photocopy shops both run late when a round closes.

## Step 6 — Reporting, fees, and hostel

Reporting means document verification and fee payment as printed on the allotment note, at the place and time on that note. Carry originals. SIMS does not print tuition or hostel amounts on this page. Ask admissions for the current breakdown before you add living costs to the counseling budget.

Note: Fee structures are updated as per HNBUMU and state government guidelines for the current academic session. To get the exact fee breakdown for this course, please connect with our admissions team on WhatsApp or request the official fee brochure.

Separate boys’ and girls’ hostels, a hygienic mess, 24×7 CCTV, trained security, and Dehradun bus routes are available. Confirm room type and whether hostel is optional for your batch. After reporting, keep the fee receipt and the provisional admission letter until the university enrolment number arrives. If a document is deferred, ask for the written last date rather than a verbal promise at the counter.
